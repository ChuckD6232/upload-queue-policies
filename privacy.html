Victoria and Maxy Upload Queue: Privacy Policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

The Victoria and Maxy Upload Queue is a private tool built and run by Doucet Services (Chuck Doucet, chuck@doucetservices.com) for one family. It moves videos from a Google Drive folder to the Victoria and Maxy YouTube channel, after a parent has reviewed them. It is not offered to the public and has no users outside the family.

What the tool accesses

  • Google Drive: the videos placed in the family's upload folder, so they can be reviewed and uploaded.
  • YouTube: the Victoria and Maxy channel, so reviewed videos can be uploaded as private and later made public by the parent.
  • Google Sheets: one spreadsheet that holds the list of videos, their titles and descriptions, and the review notes.

What is stored and where

Videos stay in the family's own Google Drive and on YouTube. The review notes, titles and descriptions stay in the family's own Google Sheet. Short review clips and still frames are stored in a private Google Drive folder that only the parent can open, and are deleted when a video is published or removed. A short-lived copy of a video exists on the processing server only while it is being reviewed or uploaded, and is deleted as soon as that finishes.

Video content is sent to Google's Gemini API for the parent's review. Those requests are made with storage turned off, and uploaded copies are deleted from the Gemini service as soon as the review answer is back.

What is not done with the data

Nothing is sold, shared, advertised against, or used to train any model. No data goes to anyone outside the family and the Google services named above.

Your choices

Access can be withdrawn at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops the tool working until it is authorized again. Questions: chuck@doucetservices.com.
